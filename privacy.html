Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

AuraStore ("we", "us") is an online shop for tech and workspace products, sold in naira. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Google sign-in details. When you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We never see your Google password.
  • Delivery details. When you place an order you give us your name, phone number, delivery address, city and state.
  • Order and cart information. The items in your cart, the orders you place, their totals and their status.
  • Technical data in your browser. If you are not signed in, your cart is kept in your browser storage on your own device.

We do not collect card or bank details. Orders are paid for on delivery.

How we use your information

  • To sign you in and keep your cart and orders linked to your account.
  • To process, deliver and show the history of your orders.
  • To send you an order confirmation email.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who handles your data

We use trusted services to run the shop: Supabase stores our database and sign-in records, Google provides sign-in, and Mailgun delivers our emails. They process your information only to provide these services.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and order records for as long as your account is active, or as needed to run the shop and meet our obligations.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing wortsock@gmail.com. You can also clear your browser storage to remove a guest cart from your device.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email wortsock@gmail.com.
